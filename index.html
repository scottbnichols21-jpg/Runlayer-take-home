Definitions

Channel
Derived from the campaign field. The lead_source field had too many spellings to trust.
Lead → opp rate
Leads that got an opportunity ÷ all leads created.
Win rate
Closed won ÷ (closed won + closed lost). Open deals are excluded.
Revenue
Sum of closed-won deal amounts, after the 100× correction.
Revenue per lead
Closed-won revenue ÷ leads created. Captures volume, conversion and deal size in one number.
Open pipeline
Amounts on opportunities still in Discovery, Demo, Proposal or Negotiation.
Attribution
Single-touch: each lead's original campaign gets full credit for its deal.
