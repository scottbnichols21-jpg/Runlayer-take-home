      Definitions

      Channel
      Taken from the campaign field. The lead_source field had too many spellings to trust.
      Source
      Marketing programs = Paid Search, Paid Social, Organic Search, Webinar, Content Syndication, Events. Referral and SDR outbound are shown separately because a customer, partner or sales rep generated the lead.
      Lead → opp rate
      Leads that got an opportunity ÷ all leads created.
      Won / 100 leads
      Closed-won deals ÷ leads created × 100. Doesn't depend on deal amounts.
      Win rate
      Closed won ÷ (closed won + closed lost). Open deals are excluded.
      Revenue
      Sum of closed-won deal amounts, after the 100× correction, as of the last close date in the export.
      Significance
      A green ▲ or red ▼ means a two-sided Fisher exact test against all other leads clears 0.05 divided by the number of rows in that table. p-values in the text are shown to two significant figures.
      Revenue per lead
      Closed-won revenue ÷ leads created. Understated overall, because many 2026 leads are still open pipeline.
      Pipeline created
      Amounts on every opportunity, including those later lost.
      Open pipeline
      Amounts on opportunities still in Discovery, Demo, Proposal or Negotiation when the export was taken.
      Attribution
      Single-touch: the campaign on each lead gets full credit for its deal. The export doesn't say whether that is the first or latest touch.
